Privacy policy.

What happens with your information when you visit this website.

This website

You can browse this website without giving us any personal information. It has no accounts or sign-up forms, and it does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Information you send us

If you call, text or email us, we use what you share to answer you and to plan your event or class.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information.

Services we link to or use

  • Google Fonts displays the text on this website and receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Links to Google Maps and Instagram take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Information saved in your browser

Your browser remembers whether you have already seen the opening video and whether you paused motion on the site. This stays on your device. This website does not set its own cookies.

Photos

If you see a photo of yourself or your child on this website and would like it removed, contact us and we will take care of it.

Your choices

To ask what information we have about you, or to ask us to correct or delete it, email hello@ourlittlevillage.com. We may need to keep some records for legal or business reasons.
